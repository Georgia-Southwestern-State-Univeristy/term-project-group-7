# **Background**
  - A high proficiency in mathematics is critical to forming a strong foundation in the fields of science,         technology, engineering, economics, and education.
  - According to the National Center for Education Statistics(NCES), mathematics literacy for the average 15       year old student in the U.S.A. consistently lags behind that of students in other developed nations (Fast       Facts: International Comparisons of Achievement (1), n.d.).
  - The National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP), an organization that reports data on reading and       math, determined that average mathematics scores amongst American students dropped from a score of 241 in       2020 to 234 in 2022. This is the first reported drop in scores since the NAEP began tracking performance       in 1973 (Fast Facts: Long-Term Trends in Reading and Mathematics Achievement (38), n.d.). 
  - U.S. students typically rank within the top 10 nations in reading literacy, but the NAEP reported a           similar drop in average score during the 2020 - 2022 timeframe. The average score decreased from 220 to       215, which represents the largest drop in reading scores since 1990 (Fast Facts: Long-Term Trends in Reading   and Mathematics Achievement (38), n.d.). 
  - Scientific literacy scores show a similar pattern, with U.S. students being out performed by several other   developed nations in average scores (Fast Facts: International Comparisons of Achievement (1), n.d.).

# **Problem Statement**
The data highlights a clear gap between students in the U.S. and several other nations around the world. The source of the problem can be attributed to factors that are administrative and instructional in nature. The instructional challenges represent the primary area where technological solutions can have a quantifiable effect on education. Additional resources to supplement U.S. students' education in mathematics, science, and reading could help students develop a deeper understanding of the subject matter, provide an immediate increase to test score performance, and deepen their knowledge and skillset in preparation for more challenging educational endeavors. 

# **Goals**
The goal of this project is to design and develop educational software that supplements the mathematics, science, and reading education for students in grades 6th through 8th. The system will utilize science based educational techniques such as spaced repetition and real world connections to help deepen the students' understanding of the subject matter. The software will also provide immediate feedback to the student while providing teachers and administrators insight into the students performance. 

# **Non-Goals**
This software is not intended to replace any current educational assessment standards or the efforts of any educators. The project’s intent is to support teachers by supplementing instruction in mathematics, science, and reading. Due to the time constraints imposed by the course schedule the software will not attempt to completely replicate the the 6th - 8th grade curriculum for mathematics, science, and reading. Select areas of each course’s curriculum will be replicated based on time, resources, and project constraints as defined by group members and course instructors. This software is intended for students not currently enrolled into an individual education program (IEP). Students with an IEP require additional learning resources and accommodations the group is unable, and unqualified, to account for within the projects schedule.

# **Features**
## **Features for All**
- Supports learning content for math, science, and reading.
- Provides a dashboard to help the student and teacher/administrator visualize the students progress, evaluate learning trends, and highlight deficiencies.
- Allow both students and teachers/administrators to access the software with varying levels of access based on user authentication.

## **Teacher Features**
- Implements a content management system to ensure teachers can create or modify instructional content.
Allow teachers/administrators to view the progress of all of their assigned students.

## **Student Features**
- Provides students with a personalized learning path which recommends lessons based on the students demonstrated understanding of the current subject matter.
- Allows the student to progress at their own pace by demonstrating their knowledge on assessments. 
- Provide student with feedback based on their performance during exercises and assessments.

# **Risks**
- The largest risk to the project is scope creep. With multiple subjects spanning several grades it’s not possible for the group to provide a comprehensive representation of each course’s curriculum. 
- There is a risk of the software reporting accuracy errors to teachers when calculating student assessments.
- Each lesson needs to ensure that students gain a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter to prevent the student from encountering a “skills cliff” and derailing future progress.
- If the content/lessons are poorly or incorrectly designed the software would not achieve its goals.
- If authentication is incorrectly implemented the students may gain access to feature specifically designed for teachers/administrators.
- The dashboard should provide an accurate and complete visual representation of the student’s performance to aid students in their progress and help teachers/administrators perform analytics.
